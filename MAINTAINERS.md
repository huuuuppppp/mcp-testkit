# Maintainers

- [@huuuuppppp](https://github.com/huuuuppppp) — primary maintainer

## Rotation & responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for:

- Triaging and responding to issues.
- Reviewing and merging pull requests.
- Cutting releases and publishing to npm.
- Ensuring CI remains green.

## Becoming a maintainer

Long-term contributors with a history of merged, high-quality PRs may be
invited to join as maintainers. Open an issue to express interest.
